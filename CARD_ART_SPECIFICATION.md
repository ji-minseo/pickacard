# PICK A CARD — CARD ART SPECIFICATION

Frozen after normalized five-prototype mobile UI gate (2026-10-04).

## Style anchors
Normalized Fool, Lovers, Tower, Moon, Sun. Warm ivory paper; burgundy/dusty rose etched vintage editorial illustration; restrained ochre/muted gold; subtle paper grain. Quiet printed quality, never glossy, digital painting, flat vector, cute, photoreal, fantasy concept, neon, 3D or watercolor. Original interpretation of traditional symbolism; no copied modern deck.

## Composition
Portrait 2:3 standalone illustration, no frame. One immediately identifiable LARGE primary subject, one strong secondary symbol, minimal supporting scenery. Broad coherent silhouettes, moderate engraving lines, generous ivory negative space. Remove tiny plants, dense foliage, minute folds, remote objects and ornamental clutter. Do not increase complexity to balance sparse cards. Similar perceived visual weight, not identical detail counts. Make the central symbol readable at 50px and all meaningful cues at 90–109px.

## Palette and typography
All suits inhabit the same ivory/burgundy/rose/ochre world. No separate blue Cups or red Wands deck. NO text, pseudo-text, numerals, Roman numerals, labels, signatures, rank/suit names or typographic borders inside generated art. UI supplies exact labels and orientation.

## Minor / courts
Ace–10: distinct symbolic scenes conveying the card meaning; never identical suit icons multiplied. Wands growth/action; Cups feeling/water; Swords thought/tension/air; Pentacles work/stability/earth. Page: curious standing learner. Knight: purposeful mounted traveler. Queen: composed seated nurturing authority. King: stable seated governing authority. Adult figures, simplified natural anatomy, no gratuitous crowded scenes.

## Gate and QC
Inspect 50/90/109/150px contact sheets, actual selection flip, result strip and position cards on mobile, plus desktop. Review silhouette, same-deck feeling, palette, line density, symbols, anatomy, pseudo-text and near-duplicate compositions. Only problematic assets are regenerated. Five normalized prototypes pass recognition and coherent style; Moon has denser lower scenery, Sun lighter ochre focus, both retain strong distinct focal points. Not all minor detail must remain readable at 50px (Fool dog is secondary).

## Delivery
192×288 and 384×576 WebP, quality83, responsive srcset, lazy loading, intrinsic dimensions, contain without cropping, local fallback. No whole-deck preload. Keep original art out of deployed output. UI reversal rotates illustration only; text remains upright.
